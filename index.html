Expected review pathway

The study is being prepared for exempt review

Exempt means a narrower pathway for qualifying minimal-risk research, not no review.

01

Adult participants

No minors are recruited, and no K–12 student data or student work are collected.

02

Educational methods

Participant activities use surveys, interviews, and related educational research procedures.

03

Confidentiality

Storage, recruitment, reporting, and withdrawal procedures are designed to limit disclosure risk.

04

One-reviewer path

The chair’s guidance identifies the study for exempt review, followed by the standard approval letter.

Final IRB work

The critical task is alignment

Every document must describe the same study.

01

Participant scope

Use consistent participant groups, eligibility rules, and enrollment limits.

02

Study activities

List each interview, survey, card sort, and validation activity consistently.

03

Data protection

Name the secure destination, retention period, access controls, and de-identification process.

04

Instrument appendices

Provide versions clear enough for participant-risk review.
